Why use Under100KB?

Sometimes an image is simply too large to upload. A school or university assignment may have a file-size limit, an online application may only accept small images, or a website may reject an image because it exceeds its upload limit. Under100KB lets you reduce the image to a target size such as 20KB, 50KB, 100KB or 200KB without installing software.

Frequently asked questions

How do I compress an image to 100KB?

Choose your image, enter 100 in the target file size field, select your preferred output format and click Compress Image. Under100KB will try to create an image that is no larger than the requested size.

Can I compress an image to 50KB or 20KB?

Yes. You can enter any positive target size in KB. The preset buttons also provide quick targets for 20KB, 50KB, 100KB and 200KB.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Image processing happens locally in your browser. The image itself does not need to be uploaded to a server for compression.

Can I compress TIFF images?

Yes. Under100KB can read TIFF images in the browser and create a smaller TIFF or convert the image to another supported output format.

Why is my compressed image sometimes smaller than expected?

Image formats have different compression characteristics. Photos, screenshots, scans and graphics can produce very different file sizes even at the same dimensions. The tool therefore adjusts quality and, when necessary, image dimensions to reach the requested target.

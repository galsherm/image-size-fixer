Compress JPG images

JPG is a common format for photographs and other images with many colors. For many photos, JPG or WebP can provide a much smaller file than an uncompressed or lossless format.

Compress PNG images

PNG is useful for graphics, screenshots and images that need transparency. Reaching a very small target size may require reducing the image dimensions because PNG does not use the same quality control as JPG.

Compress images for online forms

Some applications, websites and forms limit the maximum upload size. If you need an image under a particular limit, enter that limit as your target size and download the result.

How to Compress an Image to 100KB

  1. Choose an image from your device or drag it into the upload area.
  2. Set the target file size, such as 100KB.
  3. Choose the output format you want.
  4. Click Compress Image.
  5. Preview the result and download the compressed image.
Privacy note: Under100KB is designed so that image compression can happen locally in your browser. Your original image does not need to be uploaded to a remote compression server.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I compress an image to 100KB?

Choose your image, set the target size to 100KB and click Compress Image. The tool attempts to create an image that is 100KB or smaller.

Does Under100KB upload my images?

No. The image compression process runs locally in your browser. Your image does not need to be uploaded to an Under100KB server.

Can I compress a JPG to 100KB?

Yes. Choose a JPG image, select 100KB as the target size and click Compress Image.

Can I compress a PNG to 100KB?

Yes. Under100KB can process PNG images. Depending on the image, reaching a small target may require reducing its dimensions.

Is Under100KB free?

Yes. The image compressor is free to use and does not require an account.

Can I compress an image without uploading it?

Yes. Under100KB processes images directly in your browser, allowing you to compress an image without sending the original file to a remote server.

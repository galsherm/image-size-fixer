Frequently Asked Questions

Can I compress an image to 100KB?
Yes. Select your image, choose 100KB as the target size, and click Compress Image. The tool attempts to create an image at or below your selected target size.
Is Under100KB free?
Yes. Under100KB is free to use and does not require an account.
Are my images uploaded?
No. Image processing happens locally in your browser. Your image does not need to be uploaded to a server.
Can I compress a JPG to 100KB?
Yes. Select your JPG image, set the target size to 100KB, choose JPG as the output format and start compression.
Can I compress a TIFF image?
Yes. Under100KB supports TIFF input and TIFF output. You can also convert a TIFF image to JPG or WebP when you need a smaller file.
